Common issues

Washing Machine Problems We Can Help With

Machine Not Starting

If the machine does not power on or start the wash cycle, a technician can inspect the electrical and control components.

Not Draining Water

Water remaining inside the machine can be caused by a blocked drain, pump or related component.

Not Spinning

A machine that fills but does not spin may need inspection of its motor, belt, door lock or control system.

Water Leakage

Leaks can come from hoses, door seals, connections or other internal components.

Excessive Noise

Unusual sounds during washing or spinning can indicate an imbalance, worn component or mechanical issue.

Not Filling With Water

Slow or no water filling may require inspection of the inlet, water supply or related components.
