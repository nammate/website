Common issues

Signs Your RO Needs Attention

Water Leakage

Dripping under the unit usually points to a loose fitting or worn-out valve.

Low Water Flow

A slow flow rate is often caused by a clogged pre-filter or membrane.

Odd Taste or Smell

Unusual taste generally means the carbon filter needs replacing.

Noisy Motor

A loud or continuously running pump can signal a pressure or motor fault.

High TDS Reading

Rising TDS levels suggest the RO membrane is due for replacement.

No Water Output

No output can range from a tripped switch to a failed pump — a technician can confirm which.
